## PART A — Target Audience Research (India-first, Gujarat-focused)

> Labels: **Researched pattern** = widely observed in Indian D2C skincare buying (COD preference, WhatsApp ordering, ingredient awareness rising, price sensitivity, trust barrier for new brands). **Hypothesis to test** = my educated guess for GLOWAMOUR specifically — validate via polls, DMs, Insights.

### Segment 1 — Everyday Glow Seekers (Women 22-38, Ahmedabad + Gujarat cities)
- **Researched pattern:** Want simple routine, dullness/uneven look, tired skin from sun/pollution/late nights. First question: “Will it suit my skin? How to use? Price? COD?”
- **Problems they want solved:** Dull-looking skin, dryness at night, rough texture, need for easy daily habit.
- **Content they love:** 30-sec night routines, texture videos, “how much to use”, honest FAQs, packing/real-brand behind-scenes.
- **Hesitations:** New brand fear (“Is it safe? Will I react? Will I get delivery?”). Counter with: full ingredients, patch-test guidance, COD, Ahmedabad address, WhatsApp support, real unboxings.
- **Info that helps purchase:** Ingredients, skin-type fit, price + COD + delivery time, how long jar lasts, contact for doubts.
- **Hypothesis to test:** Gujarati captions + founder face will lift saves/shares in this segment vs English-only faceless posts.

### Segment 2 — Bridal / Pre-Bridal & Festive Glow (Women 21-30, Gujarat)
- **Researched pattern:** Searches spike before weddings/festivals (Diwali, Navratri, wedding season). Wants “glow for functions” but scared of last-minute reactions.
- **Questions:** “When to start before function? Can I use daily? What if I have sensitive skin?”
- **Content:** “4-week simple night routine before wedding/festival (patch test first)”, “What NOT to try 3 days before function”, guest/family gifting angle.
- **Hesitations:** Fear of reaction before big day. Counter: start early + patch test + honest “no overnight miracle” messaging.
- **Hypothesis to test:** Navratri/Diwali/Shadi content in Sept-Feb will outperform generic tips for reach in Gujarat.

### Segment 3 — Busy Working Women & Young Moms (24-40)
- **Researched pattern:** No time for 7 steps. Want 2-min night habit. Often oily-combo or dry due to AC/sun. Buy on phone at night (ironic + true — post Reels 8-11pm).
- **Questions:** “Is 1 product enough at night? Can I skip serum? Will it feel sticky?”
- **Content:** “2-minute night routine for working women”, “pea-sized demo”, “morning after” realistic videos.
- **Hesitations:** Stickiness, time, price-per-month. Counter: texture close-ups, cost-per-day math (e.g., “If jar lasts ~X weeks, ~₹Y/day” — only with real numbers).

### Segment 4 — Natural / Kesar / Ayurveda-curious Buyers
- **Researched pattern:** Rising interest in kesar/saffron, aloe, ubtan, ayurvedic positioning — but also skepticism after many fake “herbal” claims.
- **Questions:** “Is it really kesar? What %? Is it ayurvedic certified? Full ingredients?”
- **Content:** Ingredient spotlights (only true facts), label reading 101, “What kesar can/can’t do” honest explainer, Kesar Goti Soap journey polls.
- **Hesitations:** Fake-natural fear. Counter: show label, INCI list, Mfg details, don’t overclaim. If NOT ayurvedic-licensed, don’t call it ayurvedic.
- **Hypothesis to test:** Honest “what this ingredient does + doesn’t do” will build more trust (saves + follows) than hype claims.

### Segment 5 — First-time Online Skincare Buyers (Small-city Gujarat + price-cautious)
- **Researched pattern:** COD + WhatsApp + Gujarati support + low-risk first order drive conversion. Often buy after seeing packing videos, owner face, clear return policy.
- **Questions:** “COD available? Delivery to my town? Return if damaged? How to order without website?”
- **Content:** “How to order on WhatsApp in 30 seconds” screen recording, “How we pack your jar” video, “What happens after you DM GLOW” flowchart Reel.
- **Hesitations:** Money stuck, fake page. Counter: COD, tracking on WhatsApp, real address/city, consistent posting (abandoned page = red flag).

### Segment 6 — Men (early, secondary — don’t target heavily yet)
- **Researched pattern:** Men’s night-care interest growing but small vs women for glow creams. Keep inclusive (“for men & women” only if true) but don’t split content 50/50 early.
- **Hypothesis to test:** 1 Reel/month “Can men use night cream?” FAQ may unlock incremental buyers without diluting core audience.

**What NOT to do:** Don’t stereotype (“all Gujarati women want fairness”, “all brides want X”). Ages/needs vary. Let polls + DMs + Insights refine this monthly.

**Validation plan (free):**
- Weekly Stories poll: skin type? sticky vs light? Gujarati vs English captions? Price comfort?
- Track: which segment’s Reels get most non-follower reach + profile visits + “GLOW” DMs. Double down.

## PART B — Instagram Algorithm & Organic Growth (Simple English)

> **Confirmed (Instagram “How Ranking Works” + Creator guidance):** Each surface (Feed, Stories, Explore, Reels) ranks separately. Instagram predicts what YOU are likely to do (watch fully, like, save, share, comment, visit profile, follow) based on your past activity + post info + poster info + your history with that poster. For non-followers (Explore/Reels), it leans heavily on: what the viewer watched before + how the post performed with similar viewers (watch time, likes, shares, saves).

### What matters where (Confirmed + expert interpretation)

**Reels (your #1 non-follower reach lever):**
- **Watch time / completion:** Did people watch till end or rewatch? Keep 20-35 sec, hook in 0-3 sec, payoff at end, loop-friendly ending.
- **Shares + Saves:** “Send to friend / Save for night routine” > generic likes. Make saveable (routine steps) + shareable (tag sister/friend).
- **Originality:** Original video + your voice/text > reposted watermarked clips. Instagram has said it downranks visible recycled watermarks.
- **Topic clarity:** Say/show keywords (“night cream for oily skin”) so IG knows WHO to show it to.
- **Trial Reels (Confirmed feature):** You can post a Reel to non-followers first to test (look for “Trial” toggle on Reel creation). Use it for 1 test/week.

**Feed / Carousels (your trust + saves lever):**
- Carousels get multiple chances (if viewer skips slide 1, IG may show slide 2 later — expert observation). Great for How-to, FAQs, myths.
- Saves signal “useful”. Write save-worthy last slide: “Save this night routine ✨”.

**Stories (your relationship + sales lever):**
- Shown mostly to followers + close connections. Ranking leans on recency + interaction history. Use daily: polls, questions, countdowns, link stickers to WhatsApp.
- Stories don’t directly grow non-followers much, but they convert followers → buyers.

**Explore / Suggested:**
- Extension of Reels logic: if your Reel holds similar viewers (e.g., Indian skincare watchers), IG tests it to more of them. Niche consistency (“night cream + Indian skin + Gujarat”) helps IG find your crowd faster than random viral trends.

### What YOU control (do this) vs what you DON’T

**Control:**
- Hook, length, clarity, originality, posting consistency, niche focus, captions with keywords, covers, CTAs, replying to comments/DMs fast, removing spam, collabs, location tags, alt text.

**Don’t control / Don’t chase:**
- Exact reach numbers, virality, “best time” universal hack, hashtag flooding, follow/unfollow, pods, buying views. Instagram explicitly discourages inauthentic engagement and it attracts wrong followers who never buy.

**Realistic expectations (no guarantees):**
- New brand accounts often see 200-2000 views/Reel in first 30-60 days with consistent niche posting — sometimes less, sometimes a breakout. Success = rising NON-FOLLOWER reach + profile visits + “GLOW” DMs, not just follower count. If reach is flat after 30 posts, we diagnose (Part D in File 06), not panic.

### Growth flywheel for GLOWAMOUR
```
Niche Reel (night cream + Indian skin concern)
  → Right non-followers watch/save/share
    → Profile visit (clear Name/Bio/Pins/Highlights)
      → Follow + DM “GLOW” / WhatsApp tap
        → Purchase + permission-based review
          → New proof content → more reach
```

## PART C — Content Strategy (Balances Reach + Trust + Sales)

### 5 Pillars (rotate weekly — % of posts)

1. **EDUCATE (35%)** — Problems + routines + myths. Goal: saves + shares + non-follower reach.
   - “How much night cream to actually use”, “Night cream vs moisturizer”, “3 mistakes with night cream”, “Patch test in 20 sec”, “Oily skin night routine (2 mins)”.
2. **DEMONSTRATE (25%)** — Product in action. Goal: desire + clarity.
   - Texture close-up, jar opening ASMR, pea-sized demo, night routine POV, packing orders, “what’s inside the box”.
3. **TRUST (20%)** — Human + honest. Goal: follows + DMs.
   - Founder story, Why Ahmedabad, label reading, ingredient list explained, “What we DON’T claim”, Q&A answers, behind-scenes, customer questions (blurred, with permission).
4. **SELL (15%)** — Direct offer. Goal: WhatsApp taps.
   - Price + COD + how to order, “DM GLOW” Reel, offer (only real), Kesar Soap waitlist, festive gifting.
5. **ENGAGE (5%)** — Conversation. Goal: comments + Stories replies.
   - Polls, “Rate your night routine 1-5”, “Gujarati or English captions?”, “What should our soap smell like?”

### Formats — what to use when
- **Reels 20-35s:** All Educate + Demonstrate + Sell hooks. Face or hands + voiceover + big subtitles (many watch muted — Confirmed behavior).
- **Carousels 6-8 slides:** How-tos, FAQs, myths, routines. Slide 1 = hook + keyword, Slide 8 = CTA + “Save + DM GLOW”.
- **Stories daily (3-5/day):** Morning poll → afternoon behind-scenes → night Q&A/link. Save best to Highlights.
- **Live 1x/month (after 30 days):** “Ask us about night cream” 20-min Q&A. Repurpose clips as Reels.
- **Broadcast Channel (optional later):** “GLOWAMOUR Insider” for offers + early soap access. Only after 500+ engaged followers.

### Caption formula (every time)
```
Line 1: HOOK + keyword (before ...more)
Lines 2-4: Value / steps / story
Lines 5-6: Who it’s for + how to use + honest note (patch test)
Last line: ONE CTA → DM “GLOW” / Comment “GLOW” / Save this / Tap WhatsApp
Hashtags: 3-5 (File 02 sets)
+ Location: Ahmedabad (70%) + Alt text
```

### CTA bank (rotate, one per post)
- Sales: `DM “GLOW” for price + COD details 💬` / `Comment “GLOW” and we’ll DM you` / `Tap WhatsApp button to order 🛍️`
- Growth: `Save this routine ✨ + Share with someone who skips night cream` / `Follow @glowamour.in for simple Indian skincare`
- Research: `Poll in Stories: oily or dry skin? Vote so our next Reel helps you`

### Content rules (protects reach + trust)
- Post 4-5x/week (e.g., Mon Reel, Tue Carousel, Thu Reel, Sat Reel, Sun Stories-heavy). Consistency > daily burnout.
- 80% evergreen (routines, FAQs) + 20% timely (festivals, soap launch). Evergreen = repostable.
- Original audio or trending audio LOW volume under your voiceover. Never let trend drown your message.
- Subtitles ON every Reel (CapCut auto-captions free). Hook text ON screen 0-1 sec.
- No medical promises, no filters that fake skin, no stock “results”. Disclose “results vary”.
- Repurpose: Every Reel → 1 Carousel (screenshots) + 3 Stories (poll/Q&A/clip) + 1 WhatsApp status. One shoot = 5 assets.

### Weekly rhythm (low-budget, phone-only)
- **Mon:** Educate Reel (problem → tip → product mention soft)
- **Tue:** Stories Q&A (collect next Reel ideas)
- **Thu:** Demonstrate Reel (texture/routine/packing)
- **Sat:** Trust/Sell Carousel or Founder Reel
- **Daily:** 3-5 Stories (poll + behind-scenes + link)
- **Sun:** Rest + reply + plan + check Insights (File 06)
