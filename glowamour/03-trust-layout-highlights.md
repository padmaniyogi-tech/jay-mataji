# 03 — Profile Layout, Pinned Posts & Trust System for GLOWAMOUR

> New visitor psychology (expert model): In 7 seconds they decide — *What is this? Is it for me? Can I trust it? How do I buy?* Your grid + pins + Highlights must answer all four without DMing you.

---

## 1. Grid Layout — What a visitor should see

**Top row (most important): 3 PINNED posts** — Instagram lets you pin 3 posts/Reels to the top. They stay there.

**Pin 1 — START HERE / What is GLOWAMOUR? (Reel, 30-45 sec)**
- Title cover: `What is GLOWAMOUR? ✨`
- Script: “GLOWAMOUR is a skincare brand from Ahmedabad. Our first product is a Night Cream for daily glow. Here’s who it’s for, how to use it, price, and how to order on WhatsApp. DM GLOW.”
- Caption includes: price hint, COD, WhatsApp CTA, keywords.
- Why: Converts curious → informed in 1 tap.

**Pin 2 — HOW TO USE + Who it’s for (Carousel, 6-8 slides)**
- Cover: `How to Use Night Cream (2 Mins)`
- Slides: 1) Who it’s for (skin types — only what you confirm) 2) When (night after face wash) 3) How much (pea-sized) 4) Steps with photos 5) Patch test note 6) FAQs teaser 7) Price + How to order 8) CTA
- Why: Removes #1 hesitation (“Will it suit me? How do I use it?”).

**Pin 3 — PROOF / FAQs + Ordering (Carousel or Reel)**
- Cover: `Price • COD • Shipping • FAQs`
- Content: Honest FAQs — price, sizes, COD, shipping cities/time, returns, how to contact. If you have 0 reviews yet, say: “We’re newly launched from Ahmedabad — here’s how we protect first buyers: COD, clear ingredients, WhatsApp support.”
- Why: Replaces fake social proof with transparency.

**Rest of grid — 3-column rhythm (repeat):**
- Column 1: Product / Sales (jar shots, texture, routine demo)
- Column 2: Education (tips, myths, concerns)
- Column 3: Trust / Human (packing orders, founder story, behind-scenes, customer Q&A with permission)

**Visual rules (low-budget, high-trust):**
- Same 2-3 background colors (e.g., cream + soft pink + white). Natural daylight near window. No heavy filters that change skin tone (hurts trust + may violate honest advertising).
- Every Reel cover: large text (min 60pt), 3-5 words, high contrast, logo tiny in corner.
- No stock photos of foreign models as “results”. Use real product + real hands/founder. If using model, label clearly.

## 2. Story Highlights — Your Shop + Help Desk (9 Highlights)

**Confirmed:** Highlights stay on profile, each holds up to 100 Stories. Titles ~10 chars visible — keep short. Covers 1080×1920.

**Create in this order (priority):**

### 1) `Shop 🛍️` (MOST IMPORTANT)
- Story 1: Product lineup (Night Cream jar, sizes, price)
- Story 2: What’s inside / who it’s for (only confirmed facts)
- Story 3: Price + COD + how to order (WhatsApp button demo screen recording)
- Story 4: “DM GLOW to order” sticker + link sticker to WhatsApp
- Update whenever price/offer changes.

### 2) `How to Use ✨`
- Night routine steps (face wash → pea-sized cream → massage → sleep)
- How much to use (photo coin comparison)
- Morning after (face wash)
- Patch test: “Apply small amount on jawline, wait 24 hrs. Stop if irritation. This is general info, not medical advice.”
- Frequency: daily night (unless your label says otherwise)

### 3) `FAQs ❓`
- Price? Sizes? COD? Shipping time? Which cities? Sensitive/oily skin? Men can use? Age? Expiry/shelf life? Where made?
- Use Question sticker weekly: “Ask us about night cream” → save answers here.

### 4) `Reviews ⭐`
- **Do NOT invent reviews.** Until you have real ones:
  - Story 1: “We’re newly launched — here’s our promise instead: COD, clear ingredients, WhatsApp support, easy contact.”
  - Story 2: Early-buyer program: “First 50 buyers: share honest feedback (good/bad) and get X. We post only with permission.”
- When real feedback comes: screenshot DM/WhatsApp (blur phone numbers, get written “Yes, you can post”), add date + context. Never edit words to sound better. Mix short + detailed. Add video unboxings (with permission).

### 5) `Shipping 🚚`
- Where you ship (e.g., All India / Gujarat first?), courier partner (if any), dispatch time (e.g., 24-48 hrs), delivery estimate (3-7 days), COD fee if any, how tracking is shared, what to do if parcel delayed/damaged (contact within X days with unboxing video).

### 6) `About 💛`
- Founder story (30 sec selfie video: who you are, why GLOWAMOUR, Ahmedabad roots, promise). Face = trust for new Indian brands.
- Made in India details (city, small business). Values: simple routine, honest info, no fake claims.

### 7) `Kesar Soap 🧼` (Coming Soon — builds waitlist)
- “Our next product: Kesar Goti Soap (કેસર ગોટી સાબુ). Still in development. Join waitlist.” + Google Form link sticker.
- Behind-scenes: soap trials, packaging ideas, polls (“What size do you want? 75g / 125g?”). Polls = engagement + research.

### 8) `Offers 🎁`
- Only real offers with dates + terms. E.g., “Launch offer till [date]: Free shipping in Gujarat on prepaid.” Never fake MRP strike-through or countdown that resets daily (hurts trust, may breach ad norms).
- If no offer: “No fake sales here. Follow for launch offer of Kesar Soap.” Honesty > pressure.

### 9) `Contact 💬`
- How to reach: WhatsApp hours, DM hours, email, response time (“We reply within X hours, Mon-Sat 10-8”). What to send for faster help (order date + payment screenshot + address). Gujarati/Hindi/English support note.

**Highlight covers:** Simple icons + text, same colors as logo. Free: Canva “Instagram Highlight Cover” 1080×1920. Keep titles short so they don’t truncate.

## 3. Trust Checklist — What you MUST publish (and what’s missing)

**Publish these ASAP (non-negotiable for skincare sales in India):**
- [ ] Full ingredient list (INCI + common names) — photo of label + typed list in caption/Highlights.
- [ ] Net quantity, MRP (incl. taxes), Mfg date / Expiry / Batch, How to store.
- [ ] Skin types + age group + patch-test note. If “for all skin types” — be sure; otherwise list exclusions (e.g., “not for broken/irritated skin; stop if irritation; not medical treatment”).
- [ ] How to use + how long one jar lasts (approx).
- [ ] Price, COD availability + fee, shipping charges + timelines + return/replacement policy (damaged/wrong item).
- [ ] Business contact: WhatsApp, email, city, hours, response time.
- [ ] Real product photos: jar front/back, texture swatch, box, manufacturing seal. No beauty filters on product.

**Clearly MISSING right now (you must confirm — I did not assume):**
1. Ingredients, % of actives (if any), veg/cruelty-free status?
2. Mfg by / Marketed by, address on label, licences (Ayush/Cosmetic licence if applicable)?
3. Price, sizes, shelf life?
4. COD + shipping + returns?
5. Skin-type suitability + warnings?
6. Any lab tests / dermat review? If none, say “Not clinically tested — we share full ingredients so you can decide / patch test.”

**What NOT to do (protects you legally + trust-wise):**
- ❌ Don’t invent: testimonials, “1000+ happy customers”, “dermatologist recommended”, “clinically proven”, “removes pigmentation in 7 days”, “FDA approved”, star ratings.
- ❌ Don’t promise fairness/whitening, permanent removal, or disease treatment (acne cure, eczema cure). Use safe language: “for dull-looking skin”, “for a moisturized, glowing look”, “helps skin look...” + “results vary, patch test, consistent routine + sunscreen in daytime.”
- ❌ Don’t use before/after with different lighting/angles/filters. If you ever post progress: same light, same angle, same time gap disclosed, with consent, no guarantee implied.
- General info only, not medical advice. Encourage consulting a dermatologist for persistent concerns.

> Expert note, not legal advice: India’s ASCI + cosmetic labelling norms expect honest, substantiated claims. When in doubt, under-claim and over-inform.
