# 05 — 30-Day Low-Budget Content Plan for GLOWAMOUR

> Phone-only. No ads. No studio. 16 Reels + 8 Carousels + Daily Stories. Each item: Hook + Script beats + Caption CTA + Cover text + Keywords. Shoot in batches (4 Reels per Sunday).

**Batch kit:** Window daylight, white bedsheet backdrop, phone tripod (or stack books), jar + box + spoon, face wash, mirror, watermark-free editing in CapCut / Instagram Edits. Subtitles ON. 1080×1920 vertical.

---

## WEEK 1 — Launch + Clarity (Goal: profile understands you)

**Day 1 — PINNED Reel #1: What is GLOWAMOUR? (30s, founder face)**
- Cover: `What is GLOWAMOUR? ✨`
- Script: “Namaste! I’m [Name] from Ahmedabad. This is GLOWAMOUR Night Cream — one simple night step for dull-looking, tired skin. Pea-sized, every night after face wash. COD available. DM GLOW for price.” + jar close-up + WhatsApp button screen demo.
- Caption CTA: `DM “GLOW” for price + COD 💬` | Keywords: night cream for glowing skin, skincare india, ahmedabad
- Alt: `Founder holding GLOWAMOUR night cream jar in Ahmedabad`

**Day 2 — Carousel: How to Use (PINNED #2)**
- Slides: Who it’s for → When → How much (pea photo) → 4 steps → Patch test → How to order. Cover: `Night Cream in 2 Mins`
- CTA: Save + DM GLOW.

**Day 3 — Reel: Texture close-up (no face needed, 15s)**
- Cover: `Real Texture, No Filter`
- Shots: Open jar → swatch on hand → massage → sheen. Text: “Lightweight / Non-sticky look / Pea-sized enough” (only if true). Voiceover: “This is the actual texture — no filter.”
- CTA: Comment “TEXTURE” if you want routine video.

**Day 4 — Stories:** Poll “Your skin type? Oily/Dry/Combo/Sensitive” + Q box “Ask about night cream” → Save to FAQs.

**Day 5 — Reel: How much to use (Educate, 25s)**
- Cover: `Stop Using Too Much!`
- Script: Show pea vs coin vs spoon. “More cream ≠ more glow. Pea-sized is enough. Extra just wastes jar.” End loop: “Save this before tonight ✨”
- CTA: Save + Share with sister/friend.

**Day 6 — Carousel (PINNED #3): Price • COD • Shipping FAQs**
- Cover: `Price • COD • FAQs`
- Slides with REAL numbers only. If not finalized, write: “Price reveal Day 8 — Follow + DM GLOW for early link.” Never fake.

**Day 7 — Rest + Reply + Insights check.** Answer every comment/DM. Note best hook.

## WEEK 2 — Education + Trust (Goal: saves + shares from non-followers)

**Day 8 — Reel: Night cream vs Moisturizer (myth-bust, 30s)**
- Cover: `Night Cream vs Moisturizer?`
- Script: “Both moisturize. Night cream = your night step after face wash. Moisturizer = can be day/night. You don’t need 5 products to start. One consistent night habit beats 10 random products.” + demo.
- CTA: Save + Follow for simple routines.

**Day 9 — Reel: 2-min Night Routine POV (Demonstrate)**
- Cover: `2-Min Night Routine 🌙`
- POV hands: face wash → pat dry → pea cream → massage → sleep. On-screen timer. Voiceover in English + 1 Gujarati line: “દરરોજ રાત્રે, ફક્ત 2 મિનિટ” (every night, just 2 mins).
- CTA: Comment “ROUTINE” for steps in DM.

**Day 10 — Carousel: 5 Mistakes with Night Cream**
- Cover: `5 Night Cream Mistakes`
- Slides: Too much / On dirty face / No patch test / Expecting 2-day miracle / Skipping daytime sunscreen + face wash. Last: “Save + DM GLOW for routine help.”
- Keywords: how to apply night cream, night skincare routine india.

**Day 11 — Stories:** Behind-scenes packing + “How we pack your jar” video + countdown to price reveal if pending.

**Day 12 — Reel: Founder Story (Trust, 35s, face)**
- Cover: `Why I Started GLOWAMOUR`
- Script: Who you are, Ahmedabad roots, why simple night cream first, promise (full ingredients, COD, honest replies, no fake claims). End: “Ask me anything in comments.”
- CTA: Comment your skin concern (1 word).

**Day 13 — Reel: Patch Test in 20 sec (Trust + Safety)**
- Cover: `Patch Test First!`
- Demo jawline dab + “Wait 24 hrs. Stop if redness/itching. This is general info, not medical advice. See dermatologist for persistent issues.”
- CTA: Share with someone trying new skincare.

**Day 14 — Live or Q&A Stories:** Answer top 5 DMs on video. Save to FAQs + How to Use.

**Day 18 — Stories:** Poll “COD or Prepaid?” + “Which city should we ship first after Ahmedabad?” + Waitlist form for Kesar Soap.

**Day 19 — Reel: Morning After (Realistic, no fake before/after)**

## Repurposing Map (1 shoot = 5 assets)
- Long routine Reel → Cut 7-sec texture loop + Carousel steps + Stories poll (“Do you do this step?”) + WhatsApp Status + FB crosspost.
- FAQ Reel → Caption becomes blog/Notion FAQ + Highlight Story + Pinned comment.
- Founder Reel → 15-sec cut for Ads later (only with budget + winning organic proof).
