# 02 — Instagram SEO & Keyword Strategy for GLOWAMOUR

> Simple English: Instagram SEO = writing the words your buyer already types, in the places Instagram actually reads — so the right people find you in Search, Explore, and Reels suggestions.

**Confirmed Instagram guidance (as of 2025-2026):**
- Instagram Search looks at: Name field, Bio, Captions, Hashtags, Location, and topic understanding from text/audio.
- Captions ARE searchable — write descriptive captions with natural keywords, not just emojis.
- Hashtags help categorize, but Instagram (Adam Mosseri) says 3-5 relevant hashtags > 30 generic ones.
- Alt text helps accessibility + context. Location tags help local discovery.
- No guaranteed ranking. Relevance + engagement + relationships matter more than stuffing.

## E. What to AVOID (hurts trust + discovery)

- ❌ Keyword stuffing: repeating “night cream glowing skin night cream” 10x.
- ❌ Same caption + same hashtags on every post (looks spammy, Instagram may show to fewer people — expert observation).
- ❌ Misleading claims to chase keywords: “removes dark spots in 3 days”, “fairness cream”, “dermatologist approved” (unless you have proof — see File 03).
- ❌ Buying SEO services that promise “#1 on Instagram search” — no one can guarantee this.
- ❌ Hiding keywords in tiny text or hashtag clouds — no ranking benefit, hurts readability.
